# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- Messages no longer jump when the mouse moves over them in busy group chats.
- Delivery marks match Android: a ticked circle when delivered, an empty circle until then.
- The app starts without a console window on Windows and carries its own icon in the taskbar, tray and Explorer.
- Windows downloads are about three times smaller: they bundle a minimal ffmpeg built for Mimir.
- Small layout fix in the chat list header.

## 1.0.0

The first stable release.

- One-to-one chats, Saved Messages, groups and channels, fully compatible with Mimir for Android.
- Voice calls, one to one and in groups.
- Photos, files, voice messages and video messages, recorded on the computer or received from the phone.
- Import and export of Android backups: the computer joins your account as a second device.
- Tray icon, notifications, `mimir://` links, one running copy per profile.
- Light and dark theme, English and Russian.
- Windows installer and portable zip, Linux .deb and tar.gz. ffmpeg is bundled on Windows and a dependency of the .deb.
- Built-in updates from GitHub Releases, checked against `SHA256SUMS`.
